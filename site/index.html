What it does

Counts your swipes

Detects when Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts is on screen and counts each item you move through, per day.

Daily limit

Pick your own limit and reset time. Get warnings, and a "limit reached" screen once you hit it.

Only Reels and Shorts

Regular videos, your feed, stories and messages are never blocked.

Private by design

No account and no ads. The app never reads captions, usernames or messages, and your counts never leave your phone. Anonymous usage statistics (app opens and country) are on by default and can be switched off in Settings.

Install

ReelGuard is in closed testing on Google Play and will be available there soon. Until it is public, you can install the APK directly.

  1. Tap Download APK above and open the file on your phone.
  2. When Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
  3. Open ReelGuard and follow the short setup. It explains the Accessibility permission before asking for it.
  4. On Android 13+, if the switch is greyed out: Settings > Apps > ReelGuard > ⋮ menu > Allow restricted settings, then turn ReelGuard on under Accessibility.

Each release includes a .sha256 file so you can verify your download.

Why Accessibility?

Neither Instagram nor YouTube offers an API for what you are viewing. ReelGuard uses Android's Accessibility Service only to see whether the Reels/Shorts screen is showing and when you swipe. It ignores every other app. You can switch it off any time in Android settings. Read the full privacy policy.

Early release: detection relies on view identifiers that Instagram and YouTube change between versions, and has had limited device testing. If counts look wrong, please open an issue.
